# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The platform tracks the following business metrics:

- **On-Time Performance (OTP)**: % of flights arriving within 15 mins of schedule.
- **Average Delay**: Mean delay minutes per airline/airport.
- **Cancellation Rate**: % of flights cancelled.
- **Weather Impact Factor**: Correlation between specific weather conditions and delay severity.
