# Dashboards (Apache Superset)

We use Apache Superset as our BI tool to visualize the Gold layer data.

## Dashboards Planned
1. **Executive Overview**: High-level KPIs (Total Flights, OTP).
2. **Airport Operations**: Heatmaps of delays by time of day for specific airports.
3. **Airline Benchmarking**: Comparing airline performance.

Superset runs in Docker and connects to our DuckDB instance.
