# Business Questions to Answer

Our data models are designed to answer:

1. Which time of day has the highest probability of delays?
2. Are delays propagating? (Does a delay in the morning cause cascading delays for the airline?)
3. How much do specific weather conditions (e.g., snow vs rain) impact average delays at major hubs?
4. Which airline recovers fastest from disruptions?
